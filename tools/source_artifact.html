Paused for now while the model learns: the pre-game runs only log every prop for grading. When turned back on, each NBA day and NFL week the app locks the 3 overs and 3 unders most likely to hit (55%+ win chance, priced −150 or better) 30–90 minutes before each game, at the line then. The nightly settle grades them, so you can see whether the model is beating the books. You can also grade them now from StatMuse box scores, or mark any pick by hand.

Model scorecard: what's actually hitting

Graded best bets grouped by the model's win chance, by market and by side. "Expected" is the model's average win chance for that group; if the model is right, "Hit %" lands close to it. CLV = how far the line moved your way by tip-off (positive is good).

Is the model's win chance honest? Every prop it priced before tip (not just the 6 it locked), taking the side it rated more likely and grouping by how likely. If the 58–62% group hits about 60%, you can trust the numbers.

"Would-be picks" = props the app would lock under its current rule (55%+ win chance, priced −150 or better, model and book not far apart). "Model vs book" above 0 means its chances predicted results better than the book's no-vig chances.

Model's calls

Every prop the model priced before tip, with the side it rated more likely to hit. Results fill in once this page grades the game (about 3 hours after tip).

Model tuning: weights learned from graded picks

Role = how much recent games count vs the season. Script = how much the game's spread and total move projections. Matchup = how much the opponent's defense counts. Trust = how far the app moves away from the book's own price (0 = always agree with the book). Optimal = how much Optimal's projection counts vs the app's own. Opp inj = how much the opponent's injuries count (NBA: a thinner rotation defends a little worse, and missing rebounders leave more boards). The app learns from every prop it priced before tip (graded from box scores when this page is open), not just its locked picks: once a league has 300 graded props (or 40 graded best bets), it checks daily whether a small change to one weight would have predicted those results better, and makes it.

Spreads & totals

Model = this season's offensive and defensive ratings and pace for both teams, plus 2 points for home court, priced at Hard Rock's line. Like the props, it's blended with the book's no-vig price (30% model), so with only a few games played most sides land at 50–58%. 60%+ calls are rare and go into the 60%+ Tracker. Calls lock about 2 hours before tip and are graded from the final score.

Verdict: are spreads & totals worth betting?

Every locked call is graded as a 1-unit straight bet at its Hard Rock price. A verdict needs 50 graded calls in a group; "Worth betting" means the hit rate beats break-even by 2 points or more and the units are positive. Rule of thumb: you need about 52–55% at −110 to −122.
